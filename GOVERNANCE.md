# Governance

Reelsaw is maintained by [osbytes](https://github.com/osbytes). Today that is one maintainer,
who reviews and merges changes and makes final decisions. This file says so plainly rather than
describing a process that does not exist.

- **Decisions** are made in public issues and discussions where possible, and written down in
  [`docs/specs/`](docs/specs) or [`docs/PRINCIPLES.md`](docs/PRINCIPLES.md).
- **Roadmap**: the roadmap in the [README](README.md). A feature request is weighed against the
  principles and the research in [`docs/RESEARCH.md`](docs/RESEARCH.md); "no" comes with a reason.
- **Contributions** need a signed CLA and a DCO sign-off ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Open core**: what is in `ee/` and why is in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). A feature is never
  moved from the open core into `ee/`.
- **Becoming a maintainer**: sustained, careful contributions; by invitation.
- **Security**: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Support and supported versions**: [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
- **Name and logo**: [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
