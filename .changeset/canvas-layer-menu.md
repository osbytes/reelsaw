Layers on the editor's 9:16 preview have a right-click menu (edit text, centre, show for the whole clip, bring to front / send to back, copy, duplicate, remove) and a text layer can be retyped in place with a double-click. The Layers lane's right-click menu gained Template…, and applying a template keeps a clip's existing layers unless Replace is chosen.
