Screen + speaker framing finds the webcam tile (the picture-in-picture box of a Zoom, Teams, Meet or OBS recording) around the speaker's face and shows just that tile in the speaker pane, instead of a face-sized crop that reached into the slides, the black column or the name plate. A tile in a flat band along a frame edge drops that band from the top pane, so the speaker is not shown twice, and a webcam too small for the face detector most of the time is still framed as a stack when its tile stands steady. A forced Screen + speaker section gets the same tile, and a drag on the speaker pane still nudges it. The framing eval gained `tile` cases for a webinar recording and a corner-webcam screencast.
