Lists share one search, filter and sort bar. Users, Projects and Templates now have a search box, a single Filter menu, Group (Users) and Sort, with the number of matches and the filters in force shown above the list; the next page loads as you scroll. Projects can be searched by title, filtered by status and sorted by date, title or length, and Templates searched and sorted, in the app, through `list_projects` / `list_layer_templates`, and with `reelsaw templates list --q --sort`. Searching for a whole email address now finds just that account instead of everyone at the same domain.
