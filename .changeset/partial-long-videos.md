A video longer than your minutes cover is no longer refused. Reelsaw clips the start your minutes cover (on Free, a long first video gets its first 60 minutes), and the project shows how much is left. "Clip the rest" (also `extend_project` and `reelsaw extend-project`) processes more when you have the minutes, and upgrading does it for you. It picks up where it stopped: your clips, transcript fixes and decisions stay, and new clips come only from the new part. Upgrading also re-renders your watermarked clips without the watermark, and Download on a watermarked clip now offers that first. Sign-ups now record where they came from, even when the confirmation email is opened in another browser.
