Layer templates: save a look once (a logo pop in the first seconds, a follow card at the end, a watermark or a music bed) and put it on any clip whatever its length. Template layers are timed from the start, from the end, over the whole clip or as a share of the clip, so they stretch and contract with each clip. Build one from a clip's layers or in the template editor, apply it to selected clips (replacing or adding to their layers, with an optional re-render) or set it on a project so every new clip gets it. Also available as the `list_layer_templates`, `save_layer_template`, `update_layer_template`, `remove_layer_template`, `restore_layer_template` and `apply_layer_template` MCP tools and the `reelsaw templates` command.
