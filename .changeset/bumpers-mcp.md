Agents and operators can manage intros and outros: MCP tools `list_bumpers`, `add_bumper` (from a URL or an upload), `set_default_bumper`, `remove_bumper`, `restore_bumper`, `bumper_usage`, `set_project_bumpers` and `set_clip_bumpers`; `get_project`, `get_clip` and `create_project` carry intro/outro state. CLI: `reelsaw bumpers …`, `reelsaw project-bumpers`, `reelsaw clip-bumpers`.
