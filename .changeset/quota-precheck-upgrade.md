New Project tells you before you submit whether a video fits your plan. Paste a link (or pick a file) and it shows what your minutes cover. A video that would be refused shows why, plus the upgrade offer with its price, before anything is created or uploaded. Agents can ask the same with the new `check_source` MCP tool, and operators with `reelsaw check-source`.

The refusal for a first video longer than the free allowance now says what does fit ("Try a video under 60 minutes") instead of "0 already used passes the monthly limit". Upgrade buttons name the price ("Starter: 100 minutes a month for $12").

On a hosted instance, a project whose finished clips carry the watermark shows which plan removes it and what that costs.
