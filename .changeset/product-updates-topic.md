Product updates are an email topic of their own. Settings → Account, `set_email_preferences` and `reelsaw email-prefs <email> --product_updates off` turn the occasional email about new features on or off, separately from the note sent when projects finish. It is on until an account turns it off. Reelsaw does not send these itself: an operator's broadcast goes to the verified accounts that have it on.
