A Deleted page lists deleted projects, clips, library assets and layer templates in one place, with bulk restore and wipe. Deleting clips from a project offers Undo, Wipe and a link to the page, and the same restore, wipe and list actions are MCP tools and CLI commands. Deleted items keep counting toward storage until they are wiped, and minutes used stay counted after a wipe.
