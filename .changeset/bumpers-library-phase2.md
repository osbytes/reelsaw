Intro and outro library: keep several videos or images (PNG or JPG, shown 1 to 10 s) and choose which one new projects start with. Each project can use the default, none, or a specific one, and a single clip can opt out; changes re-render only when confirmed. The editor shows what a clip adds around its source range, and clip cards say when an intro or outro was skipped.
